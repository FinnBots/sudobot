HOW TO PLAY

  • You are Bot 2969. SUDOBOT sets five number grids, each harder than the last.
  • Fill every empty square so that each row, each column and each 3×3 box holds the numbers 1 to 9, each once.
  • Tap a square, then a number. Keyboard: arrows move, 1–9 place. Every grid has one answer you can reach by logic — no guessing needed.
  • A wrong number is not placed and costs a mistake: three mistakes in a grid end the run. NOTES (or N) pencils in the numbers a square could be — notes are free.
  • Every right number scores 10. Finish a grid for 250 × its number plus 1 for every second left on its clock (10:00 for the first, up to 20:00 for the last).
  • Your points rank on the leaderboard, and today's five grids are the same for everyone.

Free play never touches a wallet, never asks for a seed phrase, and never moves a Bot.
